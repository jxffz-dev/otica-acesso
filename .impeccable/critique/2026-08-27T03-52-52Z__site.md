## Design Specificity & Implementation Integrity Verdict

Both assessments agree: this is **not** a reskinned template at the strategy/copy/IA layer. The navy/gold palette traces to the real logo, the copy is idiomatically Ceará-Portuguese, the WhatsApp-first conversion model matches how this market actually buys, the 404 page puns on "enxergar" (to see), and the team deliberately removed fabricated testimonials/fake prices rather than shipping them (confirmed by an explicit code comment: *"não são SKUs inventados. ## Overall Impression

The foundation is genuinely well-built — clean design tokens, correct Next.js image/perf patterns, solid keyboard accessibility, a well-thought-out no-backend exam-booking flow, and a codebase disciplined enough that an automated slop-detector found nothing to flag. The gap between the site's best page (the exam scheduler, and the 404) and its weakest (three empty category stubs promising a collection that isn't there) is the single biggest issue — that gap is what will most affect real visitors and it's already flagged internally as a Fase 3 dependency. The second theme is a handful of systemic-but-mechanical fixes (one contrast token used wrong sitewide, one Button component under the touch-target minimum) that are each a one-line-in-the-right-place fix, not a redesign.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Three of six primary nav items are unfinished placeholder pages, live in production**
- **Why it matters**: `/oculos-de-grau`, `/oculos-de-sol`, `/lentes` are half the top-level navigation and the core "browse frames" promise of an optical retailer's site. Visitors clicking the homepage's own "Explorar"/"Ver coleção" CTAs land on a page whose body text says the collection "chega aqui na Fase 3" — roadmap language, not customer copy. A first-time visitor (persona Jordan) will likely read this as "the site is broken/unfinished," not "content is coming."
- **Fix**: either pull these three items out of primary nav until real content ships, or replace the stub copy with a genuinely useful interim page (real frame photography without full SKU detail) and strip any "Fase 3" phrasing from customer-facing text.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable shape` (plan the interim/real catalog content) → `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Homepage "Modelos para todo estilo" section shows duplicate/mismatched photography**
- **Why it matters**: "Redondo" and "Esportivo" style cards render the pixel-identical photo; "Gatinho" reuses the homepage's own hero portrait, which depicts round/rectangular frames, not cat-eye. For a brand whose differentiator is craft and trust, visibly duplicate product photography reads as a credibility problem, not a placeholder nicety — confirmed independently by both the design review and a source/screenshot diff.
- **Fix**: commission or license 4 genuinely distinct style photos before this section takes real traffic, or remove the section until they exist.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Systemic text-contrast failure: `text-gold-600` (#a87718) on white = 3.95:1, fails WCAG AA (needs 4.5:1)**
- **Why it matters**: this is the site's standard "eyebrow label" treatment, reused identically across 6+ files/routes — Home (×2), Contato, Lojas, the exam scheduler, and the 404 page's own button label. It's a repeated design-system choice, not a one-off typo.
- **Fix**: swap to `gold-700` (≈5.5–5.9:1) for gold text under 18px on light backgrounds; reserve `gold-600` for icons/borders where the 3:1 non-text minimum still applies.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit` → `/impeccable polish`

**[P1] All shared buttons sit below the 44×44px touch-target minimum**
- `button.tsx`: `default` → 32px, `lg` → 36px, `icon` → 32px. This hits the header's "Agendar exame," the hero CTA, the mobile hamburger (32×32), and the exam scheduler's own "Confirmar pelo WhatsApp" button — every primary CTA on the site. Notably, the exam scheduler's hand-built store/shift buttons (`px-4 py-3` ≈ 44px) *do* meet the target, proving the team knows the standard; it just wasn't applied to the shared component. Given the explicit older/less-tech-savvy persona this brand serves, this is a real tap-accuracy risk on the site's actual money buttons.
- **Fix**: raise `default`/`lg`/`icon` heights to at least `h-11` (44px) in the shared Button component.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt` → `/impeccable polish`

**[P1] WhatsApp FAB overlaps the exam scheduler's "Tarde" shift button on mobile**
- Confirmed at 390px width: the fixed `bottom-6 right-6` 56px FAB sits directly on the right edge of the second shift-selection button — right in the middle of the site's single most important conversion flow, exactly where a distracted one-handed mobile user (persona Casey) is most likely to mis-tap.
- **Fix**: suppress the FAB while the scheduler is in view, or reserve bottom safe-area padding on the scheduler card at mobile breakpoints.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] Mobile nav Sheet is missing `aria-modal`**
- Verified live: `role="dialog"`, labelling, and focus trap all work correctly (10 sequential Tabs stayed within the dialog), but no `aria-modal` attribute is present, so screen reader users aren't told the background is inert.
- **Fix**: confirm the installed Dialog primitive still stamps `aria-modal="true"` by default; set it explicitly on `SheetContent` if not.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit`

**[P1] Footer secondary text fails AA by a hair: `navy-400` on `navy-950` = 4.47:1**
- Present sitewide (copyright line, disclaimer, privacy link, city list) since it's in the footer.
- **Fix**: bump to `navy-300` (~6:1) for these instances.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

**[P2] No store hours anywhere on the site**
- Footer, `/contato`, `/lojas`, and all three `/lojas/[slug]` pages give address and WhatsApp number but never state opening hours. A user booking a "Manhã" exam slot has no idea if that means 8am or 10am, or whether the store opens Saturdays — a real anxiety gap at the highest-stakes conversion moment, and already flagged internally (PROJECT.md P-005) as unresolved.
- **Fix**: add hours to the `Store` type in `lib/site.ts`, surface on store pages + footer.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Nav active-state silently disappears on nested store routes**
- `pathname === item.href` fails for `/lojas/varjota|ipu|reriutaba` (header.tsx). Combined with no breadcrumb at this one genuinely 2-level-deep route, users get zero wayfinding cue for where they are.
- **Fix**: use `pathname.startsWith(item.href)`, or add a simple breadcrumb.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] Footer nav links have ~17px-tall touch targets**, inconsistent with the header's own `px-3 py-2` pattern on the same data source.
- **Fix**: add matching padding to footer `<Link>` items.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

**[P2] No security headers on the deployed site** (no CSP, X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy).
- Low risk for a static marketing site with no auth, but close to free to add via `next.config.js` `headers()`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

## Patterns & Systemic Issues

1. **The `gold-600`-on-white contrast failure is a repeated design-system choice** (the site's standard "eyebrow label" style), not a typo — fix once at the token/utility level rather than per-instance.
2. **Header and footer built independently despite sharing the same `nav` data array** — padding/touch-target treatment diverges between them, a sign of two implementations of "the same" component.
3. **Photography debt is the through-line behind both the P0 and the leading P1**: only 4 real content photos exist, stretched across 7+ homepage image slots, while all three product-category subpages carry zero photography.
4. **Motion accessibility is correct exactly where it was consciously built** (`FadeIn`'s `useReducedMotion` check) **but doesn't propagate** — the WhatsApp FAB's separate Framer Motion usage skips the same check.
